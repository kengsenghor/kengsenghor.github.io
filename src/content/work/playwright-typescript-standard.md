## Problem

Legacy end-to-end scripts from a record-and-playback tool needed transforming. The central test team's lead proposed Selenium with C# as the replacement.

## What I did

I disagreed, constructively. Together with a senior software test engineer, I made the case to the principal software engineers and the head of engineering for Playwright with TypeScript. Our engineering teams are JavaScript and TypeScript developers, so the framework fit the people who would use it every day.

## Result

It was adopted as the standard for these teams. The real benefit is cultural: automation is no longer only for testers. Developers can contribute too, especially when testers are absent in a sprint, so quality does not stall.
