## Problem

The team had a legacy Selenium C# suite that was neither runnable nor maintainable. It existed, but nobody could rely on it when I took over in 2019.

## What I did

I did not patch it; I redesigned it. I moved it onto a proper Page Object Model and re-architected it for multi-threaded execution, so it was runnable, readable and reusable. This role combined leadership and hands-on technical work, and this was both.

## Result

The suite became something the team could run and maintain, and the team still maintains and expands it today on the framework I built. Because the design was clean, I later brought the same pattern, together with a principal software engineer, to a different product whose team had just been set up. They started with a proven structure instead of from zero.
