## Problem

A shared team served several products with limited capacity. Fixed-length sprints fitted its interrupt-driven work poorly, so scope, time and quality kept competing.

## What I did

I moved the team from Scrum to Kanban so work flowed according to capacity instead of sprint boundaries. For one team that stayed on Scrum, I directed its manager to shorten sprints from four weeks to two.

## Result

Delivery matched capacity better, with scope, time and quality traded off in the open instead of by surprise.
